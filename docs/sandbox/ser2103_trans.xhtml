A woman named Katta-Oy, from the locale of Khatcha in the district of Khabarabad, petitioned My Most Resplendent Sovereign.

She claimed blood money on behalf of her daughter, named Qara-Oy, against Abd al-Khaliq of Samjan district, writing her claim on the back of My Sovereign's court record (maḥżar).

In response, My Sovereign graciously appointed Ghiyās̱ al-Dīn Khwāja, his royal envoy (maḥram) from among the servants of the exalted court, to attend to her case.

In the course of the shari'a litigation before Qazi Isma'il Khwaja, the aforementioned plaintiff settled her claim by the judgment of the village elders, for a payment of one thousand tangas — and [all parties] offered prayers for [the benefit of] My Sovereign.
