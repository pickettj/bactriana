Rescript
5موافق مکتوب ۳۱م ماه اییول سنه ۱۹۱۶ برقم ۷۱۶۹م شده گی از وجهً
6قاضیً کل قورغان را بقتل رسانیده گی گنه‌کار‌شده‌گانرا دستگیر نموده
7شود گفته بحاکمان دولتخانه عالی خط کرده بودیم حاکم ضیأ الدین
8قبل از خط کردن ما از روی خط کرده التماس نمودن
9حاکم کته قورغان روزیقل نام ولد امامقل را از موضع تاتکند
10دستگیر نموده بحاکم مذکور فرستاده بوده است

In accordance with the letter of 31 April, 1916, numbered 7169, with regard to the murdered Qazi of Kal-Qurghan:

  • letter: maktūb: Note the neutral terminology. Even though the Political Resident framed the request as a proshenie, the Bukharan officials translated with a more neutral term than ʿarża. (khaṭṭ and murāsala were other common ways of maintaining hierarchical neutrality.)
  • 31 April, 1916, numbered 7169: In internal Bukahran documentation, the upper righthand corner marinalia is reserved for elevatio. In correspondence with Russian official, that space is repurposed for tracking numbers and dates, both of which are absent on internal Bukharan documentation.
  • murdered Qazi: this is a great example of the "participalization" of Central Asian Persian, following Turki grammatical logic: gunohkorlardan qatl etilgan qozi

We had sent a dispatch to the governors under [Bukharan] government [jurisdiction] that they should arrest the perpetrators.

  • that: gufta: used here in the same way as deb in Turki, as often approximated to "that" in English

Before we [i.e., the Qushbegi's office] sent this dispatch, the governor of Ziya al-Din had arrested Rōzī-qul, son of Imām-qul, resident of Tatkand, at the request (iltimās) of the [Russian] governor of Katta-Kurgan, and then sent him [the accused] to the aforementioned [Russian] governor.

Narratio
11 حاضر اولیا قل نام ولد شامراد را از بازار قلعهً ضیأالدّین دستگیر نموده
12فرستاده است بتوسّط عطا خواجه توقسابه فرستادیم

Now Awliya-qul, son of Sha[h]-murad has been arrested in the bazaar of Ziya al-Din fortress.

We entrusted Ata-Khwaja Tuqsaba to deliver the accused [to Russian custody in Katta-Kurgan].
